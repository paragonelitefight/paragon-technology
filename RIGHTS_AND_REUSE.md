This private review package grants no licence. A public URL, downloadable file, hash or unsigned record does not by itself establish redistribution rights.

The draft documents Paragon's retained implementation observations. Underlying third-party data, brand names, linked pages and infrastructure may have separate terms. No third-party logos, images, full articles, raw source dataset, product technical packs or complete production Workers are bundled.

Before release, the owner must confirm authorship and permitted materials, review applicable attribution and data/source terms, and choose appropriate licensing. These are release requirements, not a legal conclusion about current compliance. A licence for future code would not automatically licence underlying data or third-party content.
