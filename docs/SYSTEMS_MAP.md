# Paragon / Sixteen-component lifecycle map

> **19 November 2026 addendum:** The numbered sixteen-component list below is the historical 16 November inventory, not a current total. The later Experimental Lab component, paragon-temporal-lab, serves the integrated browser workbenches and scheduled Living/Temporal state, with public presentation release 1.3.1. The existing gateway and Doorbell also participate in the bounded scheduled source pipeline. The supplementary paragon-agent is now documented as version 2.2.0 with eight tools, including purchase preparation and cart creation, superseding item 8’s old six-tool/read-only description. Cart creation is not payment processing. ProductSync 2.1 and PageSourceSync 1.1 have scoped acceptance, not whole-platform certification. See [connected release](CONNECTED_RELEASE_2026-11-19.md) and [dated acceptance](ACCEPTANCE_2026-11-19.md). No new live account census or complete component count is asserted.

Documentation baseline: 16 November 2026. This is a retained architecture inventory, not a fresh Cloudflare account census, uptime report or claim that sixteen Workers are active.

## Reading the architecture

Commerce, supplementary advice, publication, identity and evidence are separate responsibilities. An integration name does not establish complete synchronization, indexing, security or transaction processing.

## Discovery and publication

1. **llm-cache-headers** — selected machine-readable publications and discovery responses. The FightPulse bridge has dated acceptance evidence in four outputs. Human page bodies and Knowledge Base answers are not automatically synchronized by that bridge.
2. **robots-txt-injector** — configured robots-response customization. This is not proof of crawler acceptance or indexing.
3. **paragon-well-known** — selected identity/discovery responses and public ownership-proof handlers. Preserve signed identity material and separate proof-service keys.

## Observation and evidence

4. **paragon-heartbeat** — heartbeat-related infrastructure. No complete health-monitoring coverage is asserted here.
5. **paragon-cornerman** — source records, snapshots, selected review/comparison workflows, quotation proofs and FightPulse publication components. Full automatic rule interpretation and approval are not established.
6. **pef-canary-stamp** — canary/reference mechanism. Do not infer visitor identity or universal agent attribution from a recorded interaction.

## Product and agent interfaces

7. **paragon-product-sync** — product-representation infrastructure within its configured scope; not evidence of whole-platform synchronization.
8. **paragon-agent** — supplementary read-only six-tool MCP advice service, documented version 2.0.3. Separate from native commerce.
9. **paragon-ucp-mcp-worker** — retained custom commerce-related integration. Its existence is not evidence of an independently completed payment transaction. Do not remove it as a presumed duplicate of native commerce without dependency review.

## History, notifications and guidance

10. **paragon-price-history-workerjs v2** — catalog-price snapshots. Dated observations are not a complete immutable history or a legal pricing determination.
11. **paragon-doorbell** — discovery-notification infrastructure including IndexNow/WebSub-related work. A notification is not proof of indexing. The latest WebSub deduplication deployment was merchant-reported, without a new runtime acceptance claim here.
12. **paragon-gear-advisor** — equipment-guidance interface. Retained response demonstrations do not establish all-device reliability, unlimited quota or a verified billing ceiling.

## Feed-specific components

13. **paragon-openai-feed** — OpenAI-oriented feed component. Current ingestion, approval and visibility are not established by this inventory.
14. **paragon-google-feed** — Google-oriented feed component. Account-side approval and integration issues must be checked separately; do not disconnect existing commerce integrations on the basis of this map.

## Inactive lifecycle entries

15. **paragon-google-indexing** — retired legacy indexing component.
16. **pef-keeper-v2** — deployed but disabled; warming is off. No present warming benefit is claimed.

## Cross-cutting constraints

- Never publish private bindings, signing material, account credentials or customer records.
- Keep observation, retrieval, approval, publication, documentation and source-read timestamps distinct.
- Preserve reviewed source bytes and published hashes when adding presentation metadata.
- A historical dataset and reference graph are not live commercial inventory.
- Keep experiments away from shopping-critical paths.
- A directory entry is not endorsement or security certification.

## Documentation maturity

FightPulse has the deepest supporting report in this initial package. OhYeah has an architectural explanation and dated evidence summary. Other entries are scoped inventory notes, not complete source releases, deployment runbooks or independent audits. All statuses should be reconciled with current evidence before operational changes.
